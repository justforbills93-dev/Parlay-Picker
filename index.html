How it works

Plain-English version

1. Scan. Every few minutes we pull the current odds for moneylines, spreads, totals and (optionally) player props from every sportsbook the odds feed covers.

2. Find the real chance. Each book adds a hidden fee (the "vig") to its odds. We strip it out and average all books (sharp books like Pinnacle count extra). Betting markets already bake in team stats, player history, injuries and where the smart money is going — that's why they're the most accurate public predictor of games.

Research check. For every game we pull ESPN's injury report, each team's key players and whether they're playing, last 5 results, home/away records, this season's head-to-head, and ESPN's own stats-based matchup model. ESPN's model gets a 20% say in moneyline chances. Player props on anyone listed out or questionable are skipped, and you can skip games where a star is questionable. Starting lineups and coach-vs-coach records aren't in the free data feeds, so key-player injury status and last game's starters are the lineup check.

Fatigue check. For each team: days of rest, 2nd night of a back-to-back, short weeks in football, 4 games in 6 days, and long road trips. For basketball, hockey and soccer we also read last game's box score: who started and who played heavy minutes (38+ in basketball, 24+ on ice). Tired teams and heavy-minute players get a warning, and you can skip them in Settings.

3. Shop the line. For every pick we show the book paying the most. Getting +110 instead of -105 adds up fast in a parlay.

4. Build your parlay. We search thousands of combinations (one pick per game, so picks don't depend on each other) to hit your payout goal with the highest chance of winning.

Be honest with yourself: no bot can see the future. A "1 in 10" parlay loses 9 times out of 10. Sportsbooks win long-term because of the vig — line shopping and value picks shrink that edge, they don't erase it. Only bet what you're fine losing. Must be 21+ (18+ in some places) and in a legal betting state. Need help? Call or text 1-800-GAMBLER.
